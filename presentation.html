Entrepreneurship in India

Presentation Project

This presentation explores the key factors influencing entrepreneurship in India, a developing country. It focuses on both economic and social aspects that either promote or hinder business growth.

The presentation includes discussion on GDP, Human Development Index (HDI), government policies like GST, and social influences such as education, culture, and gender roles.
